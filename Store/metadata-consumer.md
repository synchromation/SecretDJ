# App Store metadata — consumer app (Secret DJ)

> Draft — product owner review required.

All strings below are drafts, in **needs_review** state per the
localization skill's convention for assistant-drafted copy — same
status the String Catalog uses for non-trivial translations
(`.claude/skills/localization/SKILL.md` → "Translating"). English is
the source and was written first, per the skill's "fix the source
before translating" rule; es/fr/de/nl are draft translations of it, not
independently written.

Field limits enforced below: **Name ≤30 characters, Subtitle ≤30
characters, Keywords ≤100 characters** (comma-separated, no spaces
after commas, per Apple's convention — saves characters). Promotional
text (≤170 chars) and Description (≤4000 chars) are also kept under
Apple's caps as good practice, though the task only pinned the first
three. Character counts are given per string so the product owner can
verify without recounting.

The app name **"Secret DJ" is not translated** in any language, per
the localization skill's glossary rule ("the app name... Never
translate, in any context" — `.claude/skills/localization/references/language-adaptations.md`).
Ships to `com.c-burn.secretdj` per D14 (PLAN.md) — same bundle id, same
App Store listing, shipped as an update, not a new listing.

**Subtitle** (27/30): `Request songs at your venue`

**Promotional text** (118/170):
> All-new Secret DJ! Rebuilt from scratch: faster, friendlier, same

**Description**:

> - Find venues near you with Secret DJ jukeboxes, right on the map.
> - Browse what's playing and see who else is in.

>   your activity feed.

>   easiest.

> smoother, more accessible experience — same jukebox magic, new
> engine underneath. Already got a Secret DJ account? Nothing to do —
> just sign in and you're back in.

**What's new (this version)**:
> Secret DJ has had a complete rebuild — same jukebox, all-new app.
> Faster feeds, a redesigned nearby-venues map, smoother song
> requests, and support for the latest iPhone accessibility features.
> Already have an account? Just sign in — everything's exactly where
> you left it.

## Notes and tensions for the product owner

- **App name unchanged from legacy.** No task or decision in PLAN.md
  calls for renaming the app; "Secret DJ" is kept as a safe default.
  If a rebrand is wanted, this is the file to edit.
- **"6.0" framing.** MARKETING_VERSION is `6.0.0`
  (`SecretDJ.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj`); What's New leans on "complete
  rebuild" rather than a feature list, since the underlying jukebox
  functionality is feature-parity-plus (LEGACY.md minus the Spotify/
  affiliate exclusions in PLAN.md's Scope section) rather than new to
  users. Deliberately doesn't advertise the two user-visible removals —
  Spotify integration and the listen-elsewhere sheet (D12) — since
  neither reads as a selling point; if App Review or support tickets
  need it explained, that's a support-copy problem, not a listing one.
- **Field-limit tension:** none blocking — every language's Subtitle
  and Keywords fit Apple's hard caps (character counts given inline
  above), even though German and Dutch normally run ~30% longer per
  the localization skill's adaptation sheet; both were deliberately
  abbreviated to fit rather than trimming meaning awkwardly mid-word.
  Promotional text has the most headroom (all languages well under
  170) if the product owner wants to say more.
- **Description length** is well under the 4000-character cap in every
  language — there's room to expand with screenshots-driven copy once
  S9.2's screenshot work is scoped.
